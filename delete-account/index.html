Delete Your BrainMaxxing Account

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Delete your BrainMaxxing account and its associated app data in the app, or request deletion by email.

Uninstalling BrainMaxxing does not delete your account or cloud data. Deleting your BrainMaxxing account does not delete your Google account.

Delete in the app

  1. Open BrainMaxxing and select Profile.
  2. Open Privacy & data.
  3. Tap Delete my data and confirm the permanent deletion.
  4. If your account is linked to Google, confirm the same Google identity when prompted.

Keep an internet connection until deletion finishes. If it fails or is interrupted, reopen the app and retry; deletion is designed to resume safely. The operation deletes the account as well as progress, rather than only resetting your scores.

Request deletion without the app

Email xanthoslabs@gmail.com with the subject below. This works even if the app is no longer installed.

Subject: BrainMaxxing Account Deletion Request
Send to: xanthoslabs@gmail.com

Email a deletion request

If you used Google sign-in, send the request from the email address linked to BrainMaxxing where possible. If you used a guest account, include your Backup ID if available. You can find it under Profile → Backup. Briefly state that you want your BrainMaxxing account and associated data deleted.

We may request additional confirmation of ownership before deleting anything. A Backup ID alone is not proof of ownership. Do not send a password, authentication token or one-time sign-in code. If guest access and the Backup ID were lost after uninstalling, we may be unable to identify or verify the relevant guest record; contact support to discuss what information is available.

This email link opens your email application; it does not automatically delete an account. Requests are handled by Xanthos Labs after verification, with timing communicated in the reply. No automatic web deletion endpoint is provided.

What is deleted

  • The current BrainMaxxing Firebase Authentication account.
  • The cloud profile and available Google profile information stored by BrainMaxxing.
  • Optional birth year/country, preferences, energy and saved-session information.
  • Cloud game history, scores, progress summaries, XP, levels, streaks and personal bests, including linked guest-history documents.
  • Local app progress on the device performing in-app deletion.

A remote email request cannot directly erase offline data on another device. After a completed support deletion, clear BrainMaxxing’s app storage or uninstall it on any remaining devices to remove local copies and avoid retaining an old session.

What may remain, and why

Previously submitted analytics/crash reports, service security logs, installation identifiers and provider backups are separate from the active Firestore game/profile records. The in-app action resets the device analytics identity and deletes unsent crash reports; it does not instantly erase all historical diagnostics. Firebase documents 90-day Crashlytics retention before removal begins and up to 180 days for deleted authentication data to be removed from live/backup systems. Analytics follows its configured service retention. Support correspondence used to verify or resolve your request is also separate; you can request its removal, subject to any applicable legal retention requirement. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Cloud gameplay records otherwise remain until requested deletion. BrainMaxxing does not automatically delete game history after 90 days. There is no account-preserving full-history reset in the current app.

Contact xanthoslabs@gmail.com. Please do not include private credentials in your message.

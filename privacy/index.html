BrainMaxxing Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This policy explains how Xanthos Labs handles information when you use BrainMaxxing, including its Android app (com.xanthos.brainmaxxing) and these legal/support pages.

1. Information We Collect

BrainMaxxing collects account information, game activity and technical information to operate the app and back up progress. Games can run offline, but the app creates an anonymous Firebase identity and backs up data when an internet connection is available. Optional Google sign-in connects guest progress to an account that can be recovered on another device.

2. Information You Provide

  • Your display name and preferences, such as language, haptic feedback and the usage/diagnostics choice.
  • If you choose Google sign-in: available full, given and family names, email address and verification status, Google account identifier and profile photo URL. These details are received from Google; the app does not request your Google password.
  • Optional birth year and country code that you enter yourself. Google sign-in does not automatically provide these fields.
  • Support emails and information you choose to send, such as a Backup ID, app version or a description of a problem. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes or payment credentials.

3. Automatically Collected Information

  • Game activity: selected game, difficulty, challenge seed, start/completion times, answer counts, mistakes, accuracy, reaction time, duration, scores, skill progress, XP, levels, streaks, personal bests, completed workouts and started/abandoned sessions.
  • Backup state: account/user IDs, linked guest-history IDs, an app-generated writer identifier, energy use, saved challenges, synchronization checkpoints and app version/platform.
  • Firebase services process installation identifiers, user-agent and connection information, including IP addresses, to provide services and prevent abuse. Remote Config also processes technical context such as language, country, time zone and OS/app versions.
  • If you enable Usage & diagnostics: Firebase Analytics receives app interactions and game events, app-instance/device identifiers and technical context. Analytics may derive coarse geographic information from IP addresses. Crashlytics receives crash/error traces, relevant app state, installation identifiers and device/OS metadata. Backups also include OS version and time zone while this choice is enabled.

Usage analytics and crash reporting are off by default and can be turned off in Profile → Privacy & data → Usage & diagnostics. Turning them off does not stop account, backup or Remote Config processing needed to operate the app. The app does not request GPS location, contacts, microphone access or phone numbers.

4. How We Use Information

  • Run games, calculate gameplay progress, maintain energy and show training history.
  • Authenticate accounts, preserve guest progress, back up information and restore Google-linked progress.
  • Deliver game configuration and keep synchronization consistent across devices.
  • Understand usage and investigate crashes when you enable diagnostics.
  • Respond to support/deletion requests, verify account ownership and protect accounts against abuse.

Gameplay scores describe performance in the app. They are not IQ measurements, medical assessments or guaranteed indicators of real-world cognitive improvement.

5. Third-Party Services

We use Google Sign-In and Google Firebase to provide the functions described here. Data is processed by service providers for these purposes; it is not sold by Xanthos Labs or sent to an advertising network in the current app. Service providers may process technical information under their own terms and privacy notices.

See Google Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy and Security. This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes web-request information, such as IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. These pages contain no analytics scripts, tracking pixels or cookie banners; they set no application cookies. Email support is handled through Gmail.

6. Firebase Services

  • Firebase Authentication: anonymous guest identities and optional Google account authentication/linking.
  • Cloud Firestore: private profiles, preferences, energy/saved sessions, permanent game results, progress summaries and synchronization metadata.
  • Firebase Remote Config: downloads configuration such as energy limits and game settings, using Firebase installation and technical context.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics: optional usage and crash diagnostics controlled by the in-app preference.

7. Advertising

The current app has no advertising SDK or displayed ads. Collection of Android advertising IDs and advertising personalization are disabled in its configuration. Advertising is planned for a future version; this policy and the Google Play disclosures will need to be updated to describe the actual advertising provider and data practices before that version is released.

8. Purchases and Subscriptions

The current app has no in-app purchases, paid subscriptions or purchase-processing integration. It does not collect payment card information.

9. AI Services

The current app does not send user information to an AI API and does not provide AI-generated coaching or assessments.

10. Data Storage

Progress and preferences are stored on your device using local app storage (AsyncStorage). Private backups are stored in Cloud Firestore; the database is configured in the europe-west3 region (Frankfurt, Germany). Firebase authentication, configuration and optional diagnostics use their respective Google services and are not all restricted to that database region. Older game history is fetched in pages; a local cache limit does not delete cloud history.

11. Data Security

App-to-service communications use encrypted connections. Firebase Authentication and Firestore access rules restrict access to account data. These measures reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security. Protect access to your device and Google account. Do not share sign-in credentials with support.

12. Data Retention

Cloud game history, profile information and progress are kept until you delete them or make a verified deletion request; there is no automatic age-based deletion of game history. Local history normally caches the latest 500 results plus unsent results and may temporarily include older history you request.

Deleting an account removes its active application records and the current Firebase Authentication identity. Provider-operated logs, installation identifiers, backups and previously submitted diagnostics can follow separate retention/deletion cycles. Firebase documents that Crashlytics retains crash traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before removal begins; Authentication logs IP addresses for a few weeks, and deleted authentication data is removed from live/backup systems within 180 days. Analytics retention depends on the service configuration; in-app deletion resets the device analytics identity and clears unsent crash reports, but does not automatically erase all previously submitted diagnostics. Contact us for questions about those records.

Support/deletion correspondence is handled separately from gameplay records so that a request can be verified and resolved. We do not promise automatic deletion of support correspondence when an in-app account is deleted. Contact us to request removal of that correspondence; any retention required to resolve a dispute or meet a legal obligation will be explained when applicable. Provider retention details are available in Firebase’s privacy information.

13. Account and Data Deletion

Use Profile → Privacy & data → Delete my data to delete the account and associated application data. This requires an internet connection; Google-linked accounts require confirmation of your Google identity. It does not delete your Google account.

You can also request deletion without reinstalling the app by emailing xanthoslabs@gmail.com. See the account deletion page for the process, scope and limitations. Uninstalling the app does not delete cloud data. There is currently no separate option to reset all game history while keeping the same account.

14. Children’s Privacy

BrainMaxxing is intended for people aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. Where a higher minimum age or parental permission is required by applicable law, those requirements also apply. The app does not verify age automatically. If you believe a child has provided personal information contrary to these requirements, contact us so that we can investigate and arrange appropriate deletion.

15. International Data Processing

Your information may be processed outside your country by Google, GitHub and email service infrastructure. The Firestore database region does not determine the location of all service processing. These providers apply the transfer mechanisms described in their privacy and data-processing documentation.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy when app features, providers or data practices change. The last-updated date identifies the current version. Material changes will be communicated through appropriate app or website notices.

Frequently asked questions

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile → Privacy & data → Delete my data. If you no longer have the app, email support with the subject “BrainMaxxing Account Deletion Request.” See the complete deletion instructions.

I changed my phone. How can I access my progress?

If you linked your progress to Google, use Continue with Google with the same Google account on the new device. Allow the app to connect and restore its backup. Guest-only progress cannot be recovered by signing in to an unrelated Google account after uninstalling.

Can I link Google after playing as a guest?

Yes. Use the Google connection option in Profile. The app is designed to link guest progress to your Google account and merge it when the account already has progress. Contact support if the connection is interrupted or the wrong account appears.

Do I need an internet connection?

Games can run offline. An internet connection is needed for Google sign-in, backup, restore, remote settings and account deletion. Unsynced progress remains on the device until it can be backed up.

Why have I run out of Brain Energy?

The current default is five Brain Energy per day and one energy per game. Energy renews at local midnight. Configuration may change. The current app does not sell energy.

How do I restore a purchase or cancel a subscription?

The current BrainMaxxing app has no in-app purchases or subscriptions, so there is no purchase-restoration or cancellation flow. If you see an unexpected charge, check the product and developer on your Google Play receipt and contact support without sending payment credentials.

Can I turn off analytics and crash reporting?

Yes. Open Profile → Privacy & data and turn off Usage & diagnostics. This preference is off by default. Account, backup and configuration services still operate.

Can I reset progress without deleting my account?

There is currently no separate full-progress reset that keeps the same account. Delete my data deletes the BrainMaxxing account along with associated app data.

How do I contact support?

Email xanthoslabs@gmail.com. Support and deletion requests are handled by Xanthos Labs.
